# Code of Conduct

Unmess is for people with different experience levels. In issues, reviews, and discussions, be respectful, assume good intent, and give specific, constructive feedback. Disagreement about an approach is welcome; insults, harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and sharing someone else's private information are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. If something happens that needs private attention, email the maintainer at **dineshdhanoki@gmail.com** with the relevant links and context. Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.
